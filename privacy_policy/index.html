1. Overview

Emperor Smart Solutions ("we", "us", "our") operates the Saving Challenge mobile application. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile app.

🔒 100% Offline & Private: Saving Challenge operates completely offline on your local device. Your financial goals, savings amounts, and streaks are stored strictly on your phone and are NEVER transmitted to external servers or cloud services.

2. Information We Collect

Because Saving Challenge is an offline utility, we do not require account registration, login, or personal information.

  • Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment credentials.
  • Financial Data: All challenge entries, targets, currency preferences, and saving logs remain 100% encrypted in your local device storage.
  • Device Permissions: Optional storage/notification permissions are requested solely for local functionality (e.g. daily reminder notifications).

3. How We Use Information

Any data entered into the app is used exclusively on your device to:

  • Calculate your saving progress, completion percentages, and daily targets.
  • Display statistics and streak history.
  • Schedule local push notifications for savings reminders.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties. We do not use third-party tracking or analytics SDKs that profile your financial activity.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device in IndexedDB/SQLite storage, you have total control over your data. Uninstalling the application or clearing app data will immediately and permanently erase all saved challenges and history.
